# 💍 5th Month Engagement Anniversary Website

A luxury, romantic surprise website crafted especially for your fiancée to celebrate your **5th Month Engagement Anniversary**!

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **💌 Royal Envelope Opening Screen:**
   - A royal burgundy envelope with a sparkling gold wax seal.
   - Touching the wax seal triggers an explosion of celebration fireworks and automatically starts playing the romantic Hindi song.

2. **🎶 Romantic Hindi Song:**
   - Pre-loaded with **"Ishq Mubarak (Lo-Fi Slowed & Reverb) - Arijit Singh"** in `audio/song.mp3`.
   - Floating music player with a rotating vinyl record, play/pause, volume control, and an option to upload any of your favorite songs (.mp3).

3. **📸 How to Add Your Own Photos:**
   - **Method 1 (Instant Click & Upload in Browser):**
     1. Open the website.
     2. Click **"📸 Add Our Photos"** in the top navigation bar (or click **"Change Photo"** on any polaroid frame).
     3. Choose your engagement photos or couple photos directly from your computer or phone.
     4. They will automatically save and display every time you open the website!
   - **Method 2 (Folder Method):**
     - Copy your photos into the `images/` folder and name them:
       - `photo1.jpg` (Ring ceremony / hands)
       - `photo2.jpg` (Romantic couple pose)
       - `photo3.jpg` (Date / laughing together)
       - `photo4.jpg` (Close-up / selfie)

4. **🌹 Heartfelt Gujarati Shayari (દિલને સ્પર્શતી શાયરી):**
   - 4 deep romantic Gujarati shayaris dedicated to 5 months of engagement.
   - Includes English / Hindi emotional translations.
   - Interactive **"Copy Shayari"** button to easily send on WhatsApp!

5. **✨ 5 Months • 5 Secret Love Notes:**
   - Interactive envelopes for Month 1, Month 2, Month 3, Month 4, and Month 5.
   - Clicking any envelope flips it with a 3D animation to reveal your sweet secret thoughts!

6. **📜 Royal Parchment Love Letter:**
   - A golden letter written from the fiancé to his future wife.

7. **⏳ Live Love Milestone Clock:**
   - Real-time live counter showing exact Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds since you exchanged rings.
   - Click **"✏️ Edit Names & Date"** in the top bar to set your exact names and engagement date!

8. **🌸 3D Floating Rose Petals & Starlight Canvas:**
   - Realistic gentle rose petals floating in 3D across the screen.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1 (Double-Click):
- Simply double-click `index.html` to open it directly in Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, or any browser!

### Option 2 (Local Server):
- Open PowerShell in `e:\anniversery` and run:
  ```powershell
  python -m http.server 3000
  ```
- Then open: `http://localhost:3000`
